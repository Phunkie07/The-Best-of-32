THE BEST OF 32

General Tournament Rules & Regulations

1. Punctuality & Match Start

  • 4-Minute Grace Period: Once a match is called, both players have exactly 4 minutes to connect, set up, and kick off.
  • Automatic Forfeit: Failure to start within the 4-minute window results in an automatic walkover (3–0 default loss) to the opponent who isn't available, or to both players.

2. Tournament Format & Progression

  • Group Stage (32 Players): Split into 4 custom brackets (A, B, C, D) of 8 players each.
  • Single Elimination: Every round is a direct knockout (Quarter-Finals → Semi-Finals → Group Final).
  • Final Four Playoff: The 4 Group Champions qualify for the Champions Playoff:
    • Playoff Semi-Final 1: Winner A vs Winner B
    • Playoff Semi-Final 2: Winner C vs Winner D
    • 3rd Place Match (Bronze Medal)
    • Grand Final (Championship Title)

3. In-Game Settings

  • Match Duration: 7 minutes total.
  • Player Condition: Must be set to Excellent for both teams.
  • Squads: Must be set to Dream team.

4. Draws & Extra Time

If scores are tied at the end of 90 minutes, proceed immediately to Penalties to decide the winner. NO REPLAYS!

5. Connectivity & Network Speed

  • Make sure to have a stable network connection, as the game won't be replayed if you get disconnected.
  • Your opponent takes the win if you get disconnected.

6. Fair Play & Admin Authority

Both players should always take screenshots of the final score screen, which must be submitted to the tournament admins immediately after the match.
